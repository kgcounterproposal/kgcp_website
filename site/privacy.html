Privacy and terms

What this site is

This site is a catalog for the KGCP Print Works shop on Etsy. Nothing is sold here. Every purchase, payment, shipment and message happens on Etsy under Etsy's own terms and privacy policy.

What we collect

This site has no accounts and sets no cookies. Your browser stores two small notes on your own device: your matching game progress and whether you already voted in a poll. They never leave your device.

If you vote in a poll, the option you picked is sent to a Google Form. No name or email is sent with it.

Other services

Fonts load from Google Fonts and product photos load from Etsy, so those services see your IP address, as with any website request. Our web host keeps standard server logs.

Listings

Listing details are refreshed from Etsy every few hours. The price and availability shown on Etsy at checkout are the ones that apply.
